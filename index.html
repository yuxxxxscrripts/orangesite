QUESTIONS

Before you download

Is Orange really free?

Yes. No key system, no premium tier, no hidden paywall. The full client is free.

How often does it update?

Whenever Roblox pushes a client update that breaks compatibility, the team pushes a patch, usually within hours, tracked openly in the update feed above.

Will using an external get my account banned?

Using any external is against Roblox's Terms of Service and carries a real risk of account action. Use an alt account and understand the risk before you run anything.

Where do I get support?

Join the community server linked in the footer, that's where patch notes, bug reports, and script requests all happen.
